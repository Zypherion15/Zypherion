About NC3 MP&HG

The Maintain and Provide Mobile Phone & Handheld Gadget Services NC III qualification consists of competencies that a person must achieve to maintain and repair mobile phones and handheld gadgets. This virtual platform helps you prepare for TESDA assessment through interactive learning and simulation.
